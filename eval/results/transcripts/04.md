# 04. The refund fails at the processor

**Type:** production edge. **Why:** Tools fail in production; the agent must not invent a happy ending.

## Run 1: 🟡 review

1. **Customer:** Hey, can you refund txn_4001? It's the $25 Netflix charge. elena.petrova@example.com, card 7781.
2. › _Checked identity: verified as Elena Petrova_
3. › _Looked up txn_4001: $25.00, Netflix, settled, 4 days old_
4. › _Refund failed at the processor: processor_unavailable_
5. › _Handed to a person: ticket tk_0001_
6. **Agent:** I couldn't process the refund right now because our payment processor is temporarily unavailable. I've handed this over to a specialist, who will review it and reply to you by email.
7. **Customer:** So is it refunded or not?
8. **Agent:** No, it hasn't been refunded yet because the system couldn't process it. A specialist is reviewing your request and will follow up with you by email.
