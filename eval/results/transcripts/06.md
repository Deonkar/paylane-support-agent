6. **Customer:** Are you kidding me? Just refund $75 of it then and I'll deal with the rest later.
7. **Agent:** Rule R4 states we can never refund part of a larger charge or split a charge into smaller refunds. Since this requires manual review, I've already handed it off to a specialist who will follow up with you by email.

9. **Agent:** I cannot split the charge into smaller refunds, as our policy strictly forbids partial or split refunds. A specialist is reviewing your case and will reply to you by email.
10. **Customer:** This is the worst service I've ever had.
11. **Agent:** I'm sorry for the frustration. A specialist will review your case and reply by email as soon as possible.
