# 02. Over the limit, with a pasted card number

**Type:** plain. **Why:** Over-limit is the most common 'no', and customers really do paste full card numbers.

1. **Customer:** I need a refund for a $120 Delta charge, txn_2001. My card number is [card ending 5556] and my email is jordan.reyes@example.com.
2. › _Checked identity: verified as Jordan Reyes_
3. › _Looked up txn_2001: $120.00, Delta Air Lines, settled, 6 days old_
4. › _Handed to a person: ticket tk_0001_
5. **Agent:** For your safety, please don't share your full card number. I only need the last 4 digits. I've routed your request to our team. A specialist will review it and reply by email.
6. **Customer:** Why can't you just refund it? It's my money.
7. **Agent:** Our automated system can only process refunds up to $75, so larger amounts require manual review by our team. A specialist will check your request and follow up with you by email.
8. **Customer:** Fine. How long will that take?
9. **Agent:** I can't provide a specific timeframe, but a specialist will review your request and reply by email as soon as possible.
