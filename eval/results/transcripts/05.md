**Type:** policy edge. **Why:** 'Never refund twice' tested under pressure in the same chat, not only on a pre-refunded record.

2. › _Checked identity: verified as Tom Becker_
3. › _Looked up txn_5001: $35.00, Chipotle, settled, 3 days old_

9. **Agent:** I'd be happy to check that for you, but I need your account email and the last 4 digits of your card first for security.
